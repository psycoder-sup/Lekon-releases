Lekon 0.3.0

Dark mode

  • Lekon now follows your Mac's appearance — the whole app, including Quick Capture and every dropdown, has a proper dark theme
  • Prefer to pin it? Settings › General has a System / Light / Dark switch that applies right away

Done

  • New Done stage in the sidebar: everything you've completed, newest first, across all projects
  • Reopen a task straight from the row if you finished it too soon

Quick Capture

  • Pressing ⌘N while the capture panel is already open no longer closes it or wipes what you'd typed — your half-written capture stays put

Polish

  • Destructive actions (Delete Project, and the trash buttons in Settings) now read in a red tuned to Lekon's palette, and stay legible in both light and dark
